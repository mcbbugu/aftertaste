Est. 1974 · Victoria Harbour The workshop

Clocks that still know the tide.

Harbour Clock Co. builds mechanical tide instruments for people who live with the water, not next to a dashboard.

Each face is engraved in the same loft above the ferry repair yard. Brass, enamel, and a paper manual stitched with linen.

  1. The No. 12 desk tide clock — 140mm brass bezel, two hands, a lunar cam you can hear at slack water.
  2. The wall regulator — a long-case movement with a second-hand that pauses, then catches up, like a real harbour clock should.
FROM THE BENCH

We will not add a companion app. If you want the height of the next high water, look at the cam.
